## Real research paper

Knowler WC et al.; Diabetes Prevention Program Research Group. **Reduction in the incidence of type 2 diabetes with lifestyle intervention or metformin.** N Engl J Med. 2002;346(6):393–403. DOI: [10.1056/NEJMoa012512](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa012512). PMID: [11832527](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11832527/).

`research.json` contains two concise paraphrased records based on the paper's actual PubMed abstract: methods/population and results. The full paper is linked, not redistributed. This is abstract-based research summarization, not full-paper ingestion. The abstract reports a high-risk, nondiabetic population and average 2.8-year follow-up; those findings must not be generalized to established diabetes treatment or twenty-year mortality outcomes.

Evidence quotations shown by the app are exact quotations of the curated JSON records, **not necessarily verbatim quotations of the original publications**. Users can follow source links to verify the original text.
